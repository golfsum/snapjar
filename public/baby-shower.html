The bump photos, the gift reactions, the games, the grandma-to-be tearing up. Everyone at a shower takes photos and they vanish into forty different camera rolls. One QR code on the gift table collects them all.

Why showers especially need this

1

The host is busy

Whoever throws the shower spends it refilling drinks and running games. The guests become the photographers, and their photos actually get collected.

2

The album becomes a keepsake

These photos end up in baby books and first-birthday slideshows. Having them in one place, at full quality, matters more than at most parties.

3

Faraway family can watch live

Send the album link to the aunt who couldn't fly in. Photos appear in real time, so she's at the shower from three time zones away.

Simple enough for every guest

Questions people actually ask

Can I use the same album later for the hospital or first birthday?

Each album is meant for one event. Create a new one for the next milestone. It takes about 30 seconds.

Is it okay if the mom-to-be is not the host?

Yes. The person who creates the album is the host and can delete photos. Share the link with the parents so they have it too.
