Questions people actually ask

Do my guests need to download an app?

No. The QR code opens a web page and they upload from there. iPhone and Android.

Can strangers see our photos?

Only people with your album link or QR code. Codes are random, so people do not stumble in.

What if someone uploads something dumb?

You are the host. Delete any photo.

Do photos get compressed like in group chats?

No. We keep high quality, well above what messaging apps do.

Can I use this for something that is not a wedding?

Yes. Birthdays, graduations, reunions, showers, office parties. If people are taking photos, it works.

How much does it cost?

Free for 25 photos. Party is $19.99 one time for unlimited. Pro is $29.99 with per-table QR signs.

Do guests need an account?

No. Hosts can optionally make an account. Guests do not have to.

Can I download all the photos?

Yes on a paid album, in one zip. Single photos can be saved from the gallery.

Where do I print the QR?

From your album after you create it. There is a printable sign, and Pro can print a unique card per table.

What if wifi at the venue is bad?

Guests upload when they have signal, including later on cellular. The album does not require a venue network.

Who owns the photos?

You do. We host them so invited people can see them. We do not sell them or train AI on them.

How do I get help?

Email support@getsnapjar.com. Refunds: if paid features failed during your event, write within 7 days.

Still stuck?

Album help, refunds, and privacy requests all go to the same inbox. A real person reads it.

Include the 6-character code from your album link if the question is about a specific event.
