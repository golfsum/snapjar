QR code photo sharing

A QR on the table is the whole trick

People already scan codes for menus and wifi. Snapjar uses that habit to collect guest photos: one album, live updates, no app store detour.

Create your QR album free

Why a QR beats a text that says 'send pics'

1

It is in the room

A card on the table gets used. A follow-up text on Monday does not.

2

It works on the phones people have

Camera app, browser, upload. No 'which shared album app are we using.'

3

It scales past your friends

Weddings and reunions are full of people you cannot add to a chat. They can still scan a code.

Use it at more than weddings

The same QR flow works anywhere a crowd is taking pictures: showers, graduations, office parties, quinceañeras.

Questions people actually ask

Does the QR expire?

It points at your album. Free albums are short-lived. Paid albums stay a year. Print a new card if you create a new album.

Can I put the QR on a poster or a sticker?

Yes. It is a normal code. Tape it anywhere guests will look.
