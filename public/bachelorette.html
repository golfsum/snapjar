How to actually get the photos

1

Put the QR on the fridge

The house is headquarters. A printed card on the fridge or the bar cart gets more scans than a text nobody opens.

2

Share the link in the planning chat once

One message, pinned. People add photos as the night happens instead of promising to dump their camera roll on Sunday.

3

Leave it up after the wedding

A paid album stays for a year. The couple can scroll the weekend they were too busy to photograph themselves.
