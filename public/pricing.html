Questions people actually ask

Is Party really one time?

Yes. One payment per album. Not a subscription. That payment includes the Party features and one year of hosted gallery access. It does not include lifetime storage.

What video can guests upload?

Free albums are photo-only. Party supports videos up to 1 minute each. Pro supports videos up to 5 minutes each.

What happens after a year on Party or Pro?

The included hosting period ends. The album may be disabled or become inaccessible, and its photos, videos, and related data may eventually be deleted. Download anything you want to preserve before the retention period ends. If we offer a storage extension later, it would be a separate option rather than something silently included in the original price.

Will you remind me before the album expires?

We may send email or in-product reminders when we have a valid way to contact the host, but reminders are not guaranteed to arrive. Hosts should still download important files before the stated expiration date.

Is Snapjar a permanent backup?

No. Keep your own copy of anything important. Snapjar is designed for collecting and sharing event memories, not as the only long-term copy of irreplaceable files.

Do guests pay anything?

No. Only the host pays, and only if they upgrade.
