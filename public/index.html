Questions people actually ask

Do guests need to download an app?

No. They scan the QR code, the album opens in their phone browser, and they upload from there.

Can guests upload videos?

Yes on paid plans. Party supports videos up to 1 minute and 100 MB each. Pro supports videos up to 5 minutes and 300 MB each.

Can strangers find our album?

Albums are accessed through their private link or QR code. Random album codes make them impractical to guess.

Is this only for weddings?

No. Snapjar works for birthdays, graduations, reunions, trips, company events, sports events, parties, showers, and more.
