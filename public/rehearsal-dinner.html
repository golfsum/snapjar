Rehearsal dinner photo sharing

The dinner where the real guests are, and the photos vanish anyway

Wedding party, close family, the people who flew in early. This is often the better photo night, and it still dies in camera rolls because nobody wants to be the person collecting pictures.

Why the rehearsal dinner is worth its own album

1

Everyone is closer

People sit, talk, and take candids they will not take during a 200-person reception. Those are the keepers.

2

Speeches you will want later

Not just the official ones. The side comments, the siblings, the parents before they are in formalwear.

3

A quieter QR

One card at the restaurant entrance or a link in the dinner invite is enough. You do not need a sign on every plate.

Do not mix it into the wedding dump

A separate album means you can share the dinner with people who were there, without handing them 500 reception photos.
