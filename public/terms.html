What Snapjar is

Snapjar lets a host create a shared photo and video album for an event. Guests with the album's link or QR code can view it and add content. Free albums are intended for short-term event sharing. Party and Pro are one-time upgrades for a single event album and include additional upload, video, download, and organization features.
