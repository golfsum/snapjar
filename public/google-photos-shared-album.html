vs Google Photos and iCloud

A shared album that does not require the right login

Google Photos shared albums work when everyone already lives in Google. Weddings and reunions are full of people who do not. A QR code does not ask what ecosystem they bought into.

Create a QR album free

Where shared albums fall down at events

1

The account wall

iCloud shared albums want Apple. Google wants Google. A plus-one with the other kind of phone sits it out.

2

The invite mess

You end up emailing links, chasing addresses, and still missing the table of cousins.

3

The quality tax in chats

When the shared album fails, people dump photos in texts, which crush them. Snapjar is built so that is not the fallback.

Use Google Photos for your own library. Use a QR for the room.

After the event you can download Snapjar photos and put keepers in Google Photos or iCloud yourself. That is a good workflow.

During the event, the constraint is guests, not your personal backup stack.

"We sent a Google album link. Half the table said they would join later. Later never came."
